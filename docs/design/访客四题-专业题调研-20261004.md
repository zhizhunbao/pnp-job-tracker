## 专业题:我们的三层分类

**加拿大口径核对(10-05 Frank「符合加拿大么」)**:
- 工签按 IRCC 只分两类:开放工签与雇主指定工签(PGWP 属开放工签;LMIA 那类属雇主指定)—— https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/open-work-permit.html 、 https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/employer-specific.html 。
