## 三、各省规则原文

### 安省 OINP — Ontario Workforce Priority stream（2026-07-20 上线）

**工作经验（TEER 0-3，三选一）**
- 申请前 12 个月内，**6 个月**连续全职带薪，在雇主申请审批的同一岗位（≥780 小时/年、≥30 小时/周）
- **recent Ontario graduate：3 个月**（≥390 小时/年、≥30 小时/周）
- 过去 5 年累计 **2 年**同 NOC 全职经验
- 或持有该岗位法定强制执照

**recent Ontario graduate 定义（原文）**
> 近 3 年从合资格安省院校毕业，取得下列之一：**"post-secondary degree or diploma that took at least 2 years to complete on a full-time basis"**、Ontario College Graduate Certificate、Master's degree、PhD degree

**语言**
- TEER 0-3 非技工：CLB 6
- **TEER 0-3 列名技工：CLB 5**（NOC 组 72、73、82、83、93、6320、62200；排除 726、932）
- TEER 4/5：CLB 4
- 免语言测试：同上 recent Ontario graduate 定义

**学历（TEER 0-3）**
- 非列名技工：加拿大或等同的 ≥1 年制大专学历
- **列名技工：加拿大高中文凭或等同**

**工资**
> "The wage offered... must meet or exceed the wage level assigned to the specific region of Ontario where the employee will be working and be at or above either: the **median wage level**; the **low wage level, if the employee is a recent Ontario graduate and the job offer is in a TEER 0-3 category occupation**"
> "these wage levels **do not apply if the job is covered by a collective agreement**"

**雇主**
- 经营 ≥3 年
- 营收：GTA（多伦多/杜咸/荷顿/皮尔/约克）≥$1,000,000；**列名普查区（含 Ottawa、Waterloo、Hamilton、Simcoe、Middlesex、Niagara、Peterborough、Hastings、Thunder Bay 等）≥$500,000**；**安省其他任何地点 ≥$250,000**
- 全职公民/PR 员工：GTA 内 ≥5 名；**GTA 外 ≥3 名**
- 岗位须 **full-time + permanent（无结束日期）**；季节性/合同制不合格
- 职业范围原文："the job offered can be in **any location in Ontario and in any NOC occupation**"

**流程时限**：雇主提交 job offer → 申请人 **30 天**内注册 EOI → 收到 ITA 后雇主 **14 天**内提交岗位审批、申请人 **17 天**内提交并付费

**可选 EE 提名**：原文 "You may be nominated through the Express Entry system if you either apply: with a job offer in TEER 0-3..."

### 纽芬兰 NLPNP

**International Graduate Category 资格原文**
> Applicants must:
> - Have an **IRCC Post-Graduation work permit (PGWP)** that has at least **four (4) months** validity remaining
> - Have a full-time job or job offer: in a NOC **TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3** occupation, or TEER 4 (in-demand); ≥**30 hours per week**; **at least one (1) year (twelve (12) months) in duration** with a reasonable expectation of extension, as verified by the employer; from an eligible NL employer; salary or hourly wage (not commission); meets provincial employment standards; **meets regional prevailing wage rates**
> - Have the qualifications, training, skills and/or accreditation required for the job; meet the NOC code employment requirements
> - **Be 21 to 59 years old**
> - Intention and ability to settle permanently in NL
> - Sufficient settlement funds
> - Ownership stake in employer ≤10%
> - **Working in Field of Study**: "Applicants to the International Graduate category **should hold a fulltime position that is related to their field of study from the post-secondary program they completed in Canada**"
> - Memorial University / College of the North Atlantic 毕业生可豁免专业相关要求（须另满足 5 项条件）
> - **语言测试：仅 TEER 4（in-demand）职位需要提交**

**Skilled Worker Category 资格原文（另一条通道）**
> - Full-time job or job offer in a **TEER 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5** occupation
> - **"With an employer that has a valid JVA, if the applicant does not have a valid work permit to work in the identified position"**
> - ≥30 小时/周；≥1 年期限；合资格 NL 雇主；符合省就业标准与地区通行工资
> - 21–59 岁；在加拿大者工签剩余 ≥4 个月；定居意愿；安置资金
> - **无工作经验要求**
> - 语言：`pnp_requirements.json` 记录 "No language test is required for NOC TEER 0, 1, 2, 3 job offers"

**Express Entry – Skilled Worker Category**：须已进 EE 池 + TEER 0-3 offer + **NLPNP Point Assessment ≥67 分**；原文注明 "Post-graduation work permit holders can qualify for this category"

**雇主门槛**：须在 NL 常设、于 Service NL 注册、**现管理层经营 ≥2 年（特殊情况 1 年）**；圣约翰斯地区 ≥2 名全职本地员工；**圣约翰斯以外 ≥1 名**

**EOI 优先级模型（2024 年起）原文**
> "Historically, our immigration programs followed a first-come, first-served practice. But in 2024, **demand far outstripped the limited number of economic immigration spaces** available in the province. To manage this challenge, the province has moved toward a **priority-based processing model**."
> "The criteria guide decisions. **However, they do not guarantee an invitation.**"
> "OIM **may adjust priorities during the year** to reflect labour market needs and annual allocations."
> "The criteria are **non-exhaustive and adaptable**."
> "Applicants or employers **may be directed to other pathways** if a different program is a better fit."

**9 条优先项（原文标题）**
1. Health and health-related occupations
2. Sales and service roles that support rural labour needs（并注明 "EOIs connected to the St. John's metro area may receive **lower priority** for these occupations when program usage is already high"）
3. **Jobs in rural and regional areas**（"communities outside major urban centres may receive priority"）
4. **Underrepresented occupations in NL compared to other provinces**（明列 "business and finance, science and research, and **trades and transport**"）
5. Employers with a strong track record
6. Likelihood of long-term retention in NL（社区联系、曾住 NL、亲属关系）
7. Graduates with ties to NL
8. Francophone immigration objectives
9. Strong settlement and integration supports（"as demonstrated through the **JVA and AIP Designation** processes"，含支持配偶就业）

**运营事实（FAQ 原文）**
- **"OIM does not currently have standard processing times for the NLPNP."**
- **"You have 60 days to submit a full application for NLPNP before your invitation to apply expires. It is important to note that OIM does not grant ITA extensions."**
- 提名后被裁员："You will be expected to secure new employment with an eligible employer within **approximately 4-6 weeks**... this may result in the **cancellation of your nomination and the refusal of your PR application**."
- NOC 认定："**OIM is unable to determine the NOC code of your job**... you and your employer must determine the NOC code"
- 换通道：有独立的 Policy Addressing Program Switching，须提交豁免请求 → Team Lead 审核 → Manager 批准；批准后原提名/背书取消，须重走 EOI → ITA → 新申请
- 流程含**雇佣实地走访核查**与**申请人及雇主面试**
