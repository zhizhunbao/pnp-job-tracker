# 申请步骤:在读留学生 / 毕业工签 × 九省通道(2026-10-11)

> 「在读留学生」统一口径:通道收毕业工签(PGWP)的,写「毕业后」= 先毕业拿 PGWP、拿 offer,再按毕业工签那一行;PGWP 也走不了的写「不能」。
> 依据全部是 crawl 缓存里的官方页原文(NS Skilled Worker 申请指南 PDF 为缓存页上的链接现取);AB 六条详见 [身份对照-AB](申请步骤-身份对照-AB-20261011.md)。
> 分两部分:第 1 部分官网有原句或按「要 PGWP、要 offer」规矩判得出的;第 2 部分官网没写明的,附建议判法,等拍板。

## 3. 出处(按省)

- **BC**:Program Guide https://www.welcomebc.ca/immigrate-to-b-c/bc-pnp-si-program-guide-pdf 「Any work experience obtained while on a study permit will not be accepted」(§3.8)、「two years of full-time … work experience in any skilled occupation」(§4.1(c))、Health Authority「You do not need to register for this stream」(§4.2);新闻页「The BC PNP will not be launching new student streams.」
- **SK**:Students「International students who graduated from an educational institution outside of Saskatchewan」不能申请;EWP「Study permit holders are not eligible」「Work experience obtained on a Post-Graduation Work Permit … will not be accepted」;Employment Offer「PGWP … holders must have worked for at least six months … for the employer offering the permanent job」;EOI 页「There are no scheduled EOI draws at this time.」
- **MB**:SWM「after you have completed six months or more」/「if you graduated … in another Canadian province … at least one year」;读书期间的工「will not be included」;CEP「eligible to apply … immediately after graduating and obtaining the job offer」;GIP「can apply … immediately after graduation, whether or not they have a job offer」
- **ON**:Workforce Priority「If you are a recent Ontario graduate, at least 3 months of consecutive, paid full-time work experience in the job offer employment position」/ 其余「At least 6 months」
- **NS**:Skilled Worker「Post-Graduate Work Permit (PGWP) holders working in TEER 5 positions are not eligible」;EE 本省经验「must have graduated from an educational institution located in Nova Scotia」;Graduate 职业清单 32102 / 32124 / 33102 / 42202
- **NB**:Skilled Worker Graduates「PGWP-eligible program at a designated learning institution located in New Brunswick」;2026-05-04 起 Experience 只限医疗、教育、建筑;私立学院试点「for students that are not eligible for the federal post-graduation work permit」
- **NL**:Skilled Worker「They hold a Post-Graduation Work Permit, in which case they must apply under the International Graduate category or Express Entry Skilled Worker category」;IG「Graduates from recognized public institutions outside NL must … work in NL for at least one year before applying」
- **PE**:IG「have graduated from a publicly-funded Prince Edward Island institution」;Intermediate「gained while on a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) based work permit」;EE「9 months of continuous full-time work experience with the PEI employer … IF: you hold a PGWP and graduated from an institution outside of PEI」
